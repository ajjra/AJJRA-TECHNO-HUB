💰 Cryptocurrency

Understanding digital assets and decentralized technology

What is Cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a type of digital asset that can use cryptography and blockchain technology to record and secure transactions.

How does it work?

Transactions can be recorded on a blockchain network. Depending on the blockchain, transactions may be validated through different consensus mechanisms.

Digital Wallets

A crypto wallet is used to manage cryptographic keys that allow users to interact with blockchain assets. Users should protect their private keys and recovery phrases carefully.
